What we take from this

Makes it beautiful

  1. One hero number, big and semibold, euro sign and cents smaller and lighter.
  2. Pace line 3px with a dot at today and a pill there ("€84 over"), ideal line thin and dashed.
  3. Colour means status only: green under, amber watch, red over. Brand colour just for what you tap.
  4. Tinted ground, white cards, one soft shadow or a hairline.
  5. Gradients only inside charts, fading to nothing.
  6. Familiar rows: icon, name, grey second line, amount right-aligned.
  7. Feel: numbers count up, the line draws in, drag along the line to see any day, a small reward when everything is sorted.

Makes it bland or AI-looking

  • Every number the same size.
  • Rainbow category colours, pies and donuts as decoration.
  • Tiny uppercase labels everywhere.
  • Gradient backgrounds behind content.
  • Indigo + neon, or cream + italic serif.
  • Cents as big as euros; digits that jiggle as numbers change.
