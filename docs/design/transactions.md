# Transactions
- **Position Rule.** Select mode adds a first tick column: the phone grid has a `.has-pick` variant, so every cell index shifts by one. The table has six columns (Date, Description, Amount, Category, Sorted, actions); "Sorted" holds how it was sorted, its confidence and the source (`.tx-sorted`), and a phone shows it beside the amount without the confidence.
- **Phone rows are two lines:** name, sorted and amount, then date, category and Split.
- **Queue counts** ("N to review", "N remaining") come from the queue's own query, never from the filtered or loading list.
- **Phone rows:** the name's 44 px tap box starts at the top of its cell (`justify-content: flex-start`), so its first line shares a baseline with the amount; centring it drops the amount 12 px above the name on one-line rows.
- **A row's description** is the merchant name over at most two lines of bank text (`.mer-raw`); without a merchant provider it falls back to the two-line clamp that opens on tap.
- **Fixed bars** (the selection bar) clear the chat button: on desktop and on a phone they stop one chat button short on the right; on a phone and from 721 to 1023 px (the tab slab is still shown) they sit above the slab at a higher z-index.
- **Bulk actions** run one PATCH per row and report "1 of 3 saved", keeping the failed rows ticked. Delete only appears when every ticked row is manual. Confirm stays plain so the page keeps one vermilion mark.
- **"This month"** uses the pace endpoint's `start_date`/`end_date`, never the calendar.
- **Search and chips live in the address**, replace-style: `q`, `review=1`, `month=1`, `category=<id>`, `source=ing`, `over=1`. The address is the only copy of the chips, so Back, a reload and Month's links restore them; an unknown category or source is ignored, and with nothing on the address is plain `/transactions`. Write every change in one `writeParams` call (router updates are not queued, two in a row overwrite each other); Clear removes them all.
- **A row's category** is the picker's trigger in plain text with a chevron, so it reads as a value, not a field. A waiting row says "Pick…" (`--quiet`), and picking confirms it in one PATCH. It sits in the existing category cell, so the phone grid is unchanged; on a phone its 44 px target comes from a negative block margin, so the row keeps its height. Changing it on a row the app sorted by itself (`ai` or `rule`) shows one quiet offer above the layout: always ask about this shop, or "No, just this one".
- Remember this shop for next time is a tick box under Confirm, unticked by default: a rule is only made when it is ticked. The line under it says the shop's other waiting payments follow either way.
- **Category column** is 12.5rem with no wrap from 721 px, so the longest name fits; Description gives up the width.
- **Add lives in the shell** (top row, side panel, `N`); the page has no Add button of its own, so every entry says what was added. While the review queue is open the "N to review" subtitle is folded away (the queue header says it). The count sits at the right of the view switch, in a grid scoped to `.tx-list.is-transactions` (the Merchants view lays out its own parts).
- **The Add sheet** saves with Enter in a field or Ctrl/Cmd+Enter anywhere (not inside the category list), and "Save and add another" keeps date, type and category, clears amount and description and focuses Description. Its date is the device's own day, never the UTC one. After Confirm or Skip the next review card focuses its category control.
- **Sheets with `.tx-sheet-head`** (Add, Split) pin `.sheet-actions` to the sheet's foot on a hairline, scoped here, not in `controls.css`; the sticky offset is the sheet's own padding (`--sheet-pad`, safe area included on a phone).
- **Split rows:** every control is `--tap` tall at any pointer; up to 480 px the category takes its own line and amount and remove share the next.
- **The answer line** ("Sorted X as Y. 2 more followed.") sits above the chat button, as the Add confirmation does, in the page's own persistent `role=status` region (`.tx-note`). It goes after 8 s (held while focus is inside), on the next answer or Skip, and with the page. Undo only puts back what that answer changed: the payment and the ids that left the queue with it, and only while each is still confirmed in the category the answer gave (followers also still `rule`). Anything changed since is left and named in the banner. Undo cannot restore the earlier guess, and a rule made with the tick box stays; the line says both. No Undo is offered when what followed could not be read.
