# Recurring payments
- A payment that bank files can't confirm is "expected", never "late" or "missed"; "Stopped?" keeps its question mark (No Guessing Rule).
- Detection is conservative: monthly needs three payments on days within 4 of each other; weekly and fortnightly allow 15% of the cadence; splits and standing adjustments are skipped. A price chip shows only for a rise, and only until the new price has been paid twice.
- The link row and sheet hide themselves while loading, on error, when nothing repeats, when bank files are behind, and for a month that has ended. There is no dismiss action.
