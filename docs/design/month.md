# Month (Overview, `/`)
- **Order** (the Forest brief): the figures, the pace line, "How this is worked out", Needs you, Still to pay, needs / wants / savings (`overview/Groups`), then Every category and the report link. From 1024 px Needs you and Still to pay stand in the right column.
- **The figures.** The current month with bank data shows two equal figures side by side (`.ov-pair`): **Free in the plan** (`free.free_cents`, the page's `h1`, "About €X a day", from `free.per_day_cents`) and **Left in the account** (`free.cash_left_cents`, "After bills and saving"). Under them: "N days to payday, today included." and, when the files stop before yesterday, "Bank data runs to …". Over the plan reads "Over the plan" in `--over`; a negative account reads "Short in the account". A past month shows one figure, over or under its plan; `gap` and `empty` say "Can't tell yet" or "No bank files yet" with the one import button. No plan: "Spent this month" and a link to set one.
- **How this is worked out** (`Ledger`, one fold) holds one sum per figure, each ending on its figure: the plan (planned to spend, spent, fixed bills still to come, set aside) with the per-day line and the bills ("Not a fixed bill") and set-asides; the account (money in, still expected in, spent, saved, bills, set aside, still to save) with one line saying how far and why the account differs from the plan (`free.outside_plan_cents`: income against what the plan spends and saves). Then Average day and If it goes on. Its headings are `h2`.
- **The average day and the month end** come from the backend (`pace.ordinary_day`, `free.avg_day_cents`, `free.month_end_cents`), never from the browser. Finished days only; a day with a payment of 10% or more of the spendable plan is left out; nothing is said in the first five days, with no plan or with no ordinary day. The month end is the account less the average day for each day the files do not show yet (`free.days_ahead`), and "If it goes on" names those days ("17 Nov – 23 Nov"), which is why its count can differ from "days to payday".
- **Pace line** (`month/PaceLine`): the verdict pill sits on the last day the files show. When they reach today it uses `pace.pace_delta_cents`; when they stop earlier it is that day's spending against the plan by then, with "by 16 Nov", and today is a dashed mark labelled at its foot. Over pace turns the line, its fade and its dot amber (`.pace.is-over`). The axis shows "today" only between 16% and 78% of the month and only when the files reach it. Arrow keys, Home and End read a day into a polite live region; the tooltip shows the same on hover.
- **Needs you** (`overview/NeedsYou`): payments to sort ("They count once a category is picked"), overruns, stale bank data (over 7 days). An overrun of at least €20 and a tenth of its plan gets its own red linked row, three at most; smaller ones share one amber line (one category: its own words; two or more: "N categories a little over" with each name and amount). Nothing to do: "All sorted".
- **Every category** (`month/Categories`, inside `.ov-fold`): the three groups are sections of the one card, split by a hairline (top on a phone, left from 720 px), never cards. Each heading adds its share of the month's income and the household's aim from the target split. A category with no plan has no meter.
- **Untouched rows fold.** In each group, rows with nothing spent (two or more) sit behind one "N untouched · €X planned" line (`.untouched`) after the rest; it opens by itself while one of its rows is open.
- **Open category payments** (`.cat-tx`) are date, description (wraps), amount (right, never wraps) on one line and the category chip full width under. Keep the three-column grid in step with the three first children. An opened category ends with "All payments" (`?category=<id>`, plus `&month=1` only for the current financial month), ink and underlined.
- **Order is frozen while a row is open.** Never re-sort categories under an open editor; they sort again on close and on a month change.
- **"Paid"** on a row needs only status `ok`. **"Ahead"** follows the projection rules.
- **A month change keeps the last month dimmed** (`aria-busy`) under a stable month bar; never swap the page for a skeleton. The month arrows are `--tap` on a phone; "Next month" on the current month is disabled and faded.
